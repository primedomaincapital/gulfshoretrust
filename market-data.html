Naples does not just have wealthy residents. It imports them, year after year, and the trust business follows the money.

Collier County's population growth in recent years has come entirely from migration: deaths exceeded births by about 1,100 in the latest Census year, while roughly 6,200 new residents arrived. Who arrives matters more than how many. IRS county-to-county data put the average income of arriving households near $283,000, against a county median household income of about $90,000 — newcomers earning roughly three times the resident median.

That inflow supports a trust industry with real scale. Florida Trust Wealth Management, an independent state-chartered trust company with a Naples office, publicly cites more than $5 billion in assets under management, with a separate company release putting the figure at approximately $6 billion. The range itself is informative: trust-company AUM figures move with markets and with how recently a release was written, so this page shows both rather than picking one.

The housing data completes the picture. The Naples Area Board of REALTORS reported a full-year 2025 overall median closed price of $594,500, up 79% from the $332,000 pre-pandemic median of 2019, and sales of homes priced at $5 million and above rose 16.6% in 2025. Trust work — estate settlement, fiduciary administration, family governance — tracks the stock of wealth, not the flow of sales, but the two move together in a market where the buyer base keeps getting richer.

Reading the numbers

There is no public aggregate for total trust assets held in Naples or Southwest Florida. Bank trust departments report through regulatory filings, but no published total for the region was verifiable in this session, so this page uses the largest local independent trust company's published AUM and the demographic and housing data around it instead of estimating a regional total.

AUM figures from company releases are self-reported and move with markets. The $5 billion and $6 billion figures for the same firm come from different releases at different dates; both are shown so the reader can see the range rather than a false point estimate.

Methodology

Included: published AUM for the largest Southwest Florida independent trust company with a Naples office, Census income and demographic figures as summarized from ACS data, IRS migration income figures as summarized publicly, and Naples Area Board of REALTORS housing figures as reported publicly. Excluded: any regional trust-AUM total (none verifiable), bank-level trust department figures from filings that could not be verified to a public page in this session, and Sarasota-specific figures (see gaps).

Disclaimer. Public figures change as new reports are published, and estimates from different firms are built on different definitions, so a single headline total should never be read as a precise count. Figures on this page are aggregated public estimates compiled for information only. They are not investment, legal, tax, or professional advice, and they do not predict future results. Where sources disagree, both figures are shown and the difference is explained rather than averaged away.
